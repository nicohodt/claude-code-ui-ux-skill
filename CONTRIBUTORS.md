## How to get on this list

1. Pick anything from [what we need right now](README.md#-what-we-need-right-now), a [good first issue](https://github.com/nicohodt/claude-code-ui-ux-skill/issues?q=is%3Aissue+is%3Aopen+label%3A%22good+first+issue%22), or your own idea.
2. Open a PR. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) — the fast path takes about five minutes and doesn't require cloning the repo.
3. Add your line above in the same PR.

Contributions of every size count equally here. There is no minimum bar.
